Deadlock Decoded

How the best Deadlock players actually play, explained with match data.

What we make

Every day, short videos that break down a winning game from a top leaderboard player:

  • Farm route: where the player went during the first 10 minutes, second by second.
  • Heatmap: where they spent the whole game, lane by lane.
  • Build: every item, in the order and at the minute they bought it.

The videos are generated from public match data provided by deadlock-api.com. Music is original and generated for the channel.

About the uploader app

The channel uses a small private tool, "Deadlock Decoded", that uploads these videos to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. Details are in the privacy policy.
