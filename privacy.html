Deadlock Decoded

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

What the app is

"Deadlock Decoded" is a private tool used by the owner of the Deadlock Decoded YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel and to send them as drafts to the owner's own TikTok account. It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service and no other user signs in to it.

Google data the app uses

The app asks for a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the YouTube channel of the account that authorized it.

  • It uses this permission only to upload the channel's own videos, with their title, description and tags.
  • It does not read, collect or store any other YouTube or Google account data: no email, contacts, watch history, comments or analytics.
  • The access token from Google is stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file. It is never sent anywhere other than Google's own servers.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok data the app uses

The app uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API with two permissions: user.info.basic and video.upload.

  • It uses them only to send the channel's own videos as drafts to the TikTok inbox of the account that authorized it. The owner then edits and posts each draft in the TikTok app.
  • It does not read, collect or store any other TikTok data: no followers, messages, comments, videos of other users or analytics.
  • The TikTok access token is stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file. It is never sent anywhere other than TikTok's own servers.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Match data shown in the videos

The videos use public Deadlock match statistics from deadlock-api.com, including public player names as they appear on the in-game leaderboard. No private information about players is collected.

Revoking access

The access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions for YouTube, and from Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions in the TikTok app for TikTok. Deleting the local token files also stops the app from uploading.
